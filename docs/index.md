# Welcome to the UPSC PYQ Tracker

An open-source, highly structured repository for UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE) Mains Previous Year Questions (PYQs). 

This platform is designed to move beyond generic answers by providing **high-density solution frameworks**, examiner-ready data points, and structured value additions mapped directly to the syllabus.

!!! tip "Search & Navigate"
    Use the **Search bar** at the top right to instantly find specific topics, thinkers, or keywords (e.g., "Sanskritization", "Balanced Polymorphism", "Self-Help Groups") across all years and papers.

## 🎯 Anatomy of Our Solution Frameworks

To maximize scoring potential, every PYQ solution here is deconstructed into an analytical, easily replicable format:

1. **Introduction:** Precise definitions, origin of concepts (e.g., *E.B. Tylor's* definitions), or relevant constitutional/historical context.
2. **Body (The Core Demand):** 
    * **Micro-headings** directly addressing the question's distinct parts.
    * **Value Addition:** Explicit integration of data points, specialized italicized terminology, and committee reports (e.g., Xaxa Committee, ARC).
    * **Scholar Perspectives:** Highlighting key thinkers and ethnographic case studies.
3. **Conclusion:** Forward-looking synthesis, contemporary relevance, or multi-disciplinary linkages.
